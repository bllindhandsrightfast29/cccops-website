Program Description

Triple C Consulting, LLC sends operational and account-related text (SMS) messages to existing clients, partners, and contacts. Messages may include appointment and capability-brief confirmations, support follow-ups, and account notifications. This is not a marketing program; we do not send promotional or advertising text messages.

How You Opt In

Consent to receive text messages is obtained verbally or in person during a phone call or meeting with a Triple C Consulting representative, or through an existing business relationship in which you have provided your mobile number for contact. At the time of opt-in, the representative states that we can send text updates about your request, briefing, or account; that message and data rates may apply; and that you can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is logged. We do not add any number without consent.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and is typically only a few messages per month, based on your active requests and account activity.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Triple C Consulting does not charge for the messages themselves.

Opt-Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send one confirmation message and then stop sending SMS messages. You may opt back in at any time by contacting us.

Help

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at (509) 903-6285 or consultingbytriplec@gmail.com.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our full Privacy Policy.
