Triple C Consulting, LLC · Last updated: July 8, 2026

Triple C Consulting, LLC ("Triple C Consulting," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website at cccops.com and to communications we exchange with you, including phone calls and text (SMS) messages.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide — such as your name, organization, email address, and phone number — when you contact us, request a capability brief, or engage our services.
  • Communications data — the content of messages, calls, and correspondence you exchange with us.
  • Basic technical data — standard web log information when you visit our site.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To send operational and account-related communications (for example, confirmations, follow-ups, and notifications) to contacts who have consented to receive them.
  • To meet legal, contracting, and compliance obligations.

3. SMS / Text Messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

If you consent to receive text messages from Triple C Consulting, we use your mobile number only to send the operational and account-related messages you agreed to receive. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for assistance. See our SMS Terms for details on how consent is obtained and managed.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, communications and IT providers) strictly to perform services on our behalf, and when required by law. As stated above, mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, and we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of communications at any time, by contacting us using the details below.
